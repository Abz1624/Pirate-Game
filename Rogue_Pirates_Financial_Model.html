Gold / Rum review

All unspent run Gold is now banked on death. The revised design lowers passive production, raises long-term upgrade costs and adds account-bound prestige sinks. Ads award at most two small post-run caches daily and never revive a run or affect its score.

Sources and limitations

All engagement, payer, spend, eCPM, compute performance and operating budgets are assumptions. Baseline excludes unsigned sponsorship, player trading and recurring Poki income. Costs include 25% hosting contingency, two budgeted Unity Pro seats and $5,000 operating budget; change these to your actuals. No income tax or payment-withholding calculation without a jurisdiction. Monthly operating profit is not cash flow or recovery of initial development.
