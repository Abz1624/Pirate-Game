# M2 onboarding playtest checklist

Use after Saltwind slice is playable on deterministic foundation.

## Session setup
- [ ] Fresh local guest profile (no public account)
- [ ] Captain: Rook
- [ ] Record seed and build version

## Comprehension (target ≥80% can explain without staff)
- [ ] What is the Treasury Beacon objective?
- [ ] What happens after boss victory?
- [ ] Why did you die vs why did you succeed?
- [ ] Difference between run gold and saved treasury gold

## Behavioral targets
- [ ] First conquest completion ≥70% (sample ≥10)
- [ ] Immediate weak-build retry ≥40%
- [ ] Post-boss survival understood (combat continues)

## Accessibility/readability
- [ ] Telegraphs readable at minimum device quality tier
- [ ] HUD readable at 1280×720 landscape
- [ ] Color-independent danger cues present

## Balance observations
- [ ] Dominant mandatory picks ≤65%
- [ ] Dead offers ≤15%
- [ ] Unwinnable seeds documented

Record results in `plan/balance_reports/m2_<date>.md`.
