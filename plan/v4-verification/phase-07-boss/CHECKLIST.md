## What M7 delivers

**Stage content** (`Runtime/Stage/StageContentModule.cs`): port of the V3 paths of POIManager and POIInteractable, driven by the stage layout's POIs (placement stays in the shared StageBuilder on the map stream). Same rules, texts, random draws and order:
- **Chests**: price quote, the loot plan on the loot stream (24 attempts), the item, a refund when the inventory is full, the progressive spawn when gold-triggered chests are on (Saltwind spawns all at the start).
- **Repair Wreck** heals a quarter of max hull (refused at full hull).
- **Powder Cache**: sailing within 8 m starts an ambush of 3 through the V4 encounter executor. Its payout (an item from the chest pool on the map stream, 20 gold) is ported, but, as in V3, it is never reached in play: the hidden cache is never targeted again (R-97; fix behind `powderCachePaysWhenCleared`, off).
- **Signal Buoy**: charge 8 s inside the 4 m circle, then a reward drawn on the map stream.
- **Treasure Chart** reveals the 3 nearest chests and gives the Beacon's bearing. **Lighthouse of Fortune** (not placed in Saltwind): damage ×1.08.
- **Treasury Beacon**: hold 0.7 s, telegraph 0.7 s, boss summon; boss victory (Marks, telemetry, objective, home seed, toast, back to Playing; a same-tick death still counts as the win).

**Beacon discovery** (`BeaconDiscovery.cs`, port of V3DiscoverySystem): explored cells, POIs within 24 m, chests revealed by the Treasure Chart, the Beacon's reveal timeline.

**Interaction** (`InteractionModel.cs`, port of V3InteractionController's logic; `InteractionPromptPresenter` draws the panel): nearest target, the chest Take/Skip popup that pauses the run (POIChoice), Signal Buoy charge text, instant landmarks, the Beacon hold (E or the touch USE button).

**Encounters** (`Runtime/Spawning/EncounterExecutor.cs`, port of EncounterSpawner's trigger queue): the Beacon boss and the Powder Cache spawn through V4, ignoring the ambient soft cap, retrying every 0.35 s, failing after 10 s (`requiredEncounterTimeoutSeconds`, now live). V3's queue waited during miniboss arenas; V4's does not unless `timeline.saltwind.encountersWaitDuringArenas` is on (the v3-parity preset turns it on). Records stay in the static RunEncounterTracker until M8, because the legacy enemy world's kill path completes them there.

**Barnacle King** (`BossDirector.cs`, port of BarnacleKingBossAttackDirector + BarnacleKingBossAttackLogic; config `boss.barnacle_king`): phase thresholds 0.65/0.3, enrage at 0.3, per-phase rotations, the phase-3 second charge, telegraph/execute/recovery times, enraged recovery ×0.75, aimed spread, marked bombardment, shell slam and the committed charge, the telegraph toasts; `BossTelegraphPresenter` draws the marked circle. With the default config the director's state sequence equals V3's frame for frame (tests compare them over 90 s fights).

**Config**: `StageContentConfig` (`stage.saltwind`) with V3's values; it now owns the stage profile's placement counts, chest prices and landmark values (`LegacyConfigMapping.ApplyStage`); `BossConfig` (`boss.barnacle_king`). Generated by `V3ConfigMigrator.ApplyM7` and `V4BossConfigGenerator`.

**Frame order.** Each V4 tick runs where V3's piece ran: the stage manager in POIManager's scene slot (a LegacyUpdateSlot took POIManager's file ID, `V4SceneSetup.ApplyM7`); each marker's tick on the marker object, created at the same moment and in the same order as V3's markers; discovery and interaction on objects created where V3DiscoverySystem and V3InteractionController were created; the boss director first in the legacy world tick; the encounter queue at the start of the ambient slot (EncounterSpawner.Update's place). Boss victory: V3's kill path called POIManager right before raising TriggerBus.Kill; V4 listens to that event (first subscriber) and checks the same condition.

**Scene**: POIManager removed from V4SaltwindRun and V4ModuleLab; EncounterSpawner switched off (it stays as the target Dead Calm writes to until M9, register D4).

**Events**: the stage module publishes ChestOpened, LandmarkUsed, BeaconState and BossState itself (M6 polled them).

**Tuning Console → Landmarks, Beacon & Boss**: state, Reveal Beacon, Summon boss (and the B key, dev builds only), set boss health %.
