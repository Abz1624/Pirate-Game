# Channel gates (M0-006)

| Channel | Ads | IAP cosmetics | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steam | **No** | Yes (account-bound) | No ad model assumed |
| iOS | Rewarded only (approved placements) | Yes | ATT/consent required |
| Android | Rewarded only | Yes | UMP/consent required |
| CrazyGames / Poki | Portal rules | Limited | Interstitial caps per portal |
| Direct web | Optional rewarded | Yes | Cookie/consent banner |

## Non-negotiable commerce rules

- No blockchain, wallets, player trading
- No direct Gold/Rum purchase
- No combat-interrupting ads, revives, or score multipliers
- Paid cosmetics must not confer statistically meaningful score advantage

## Gate status

All production channel integrations remain **blocked** until M6 sandbox validation and owner authorization.
