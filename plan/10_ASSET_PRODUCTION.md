# 10 — Art, VFX, Audio and Production Assets

Every asset record stores ID, owner/status, editable source/export, author/license/attribution, units/pivot/sockets/rig/materials, budgets, LOD/quality variants, import settings, GUIDs, thumbnails/localization and acceptance screenshots at gameplay scale.

## Required inventory

- Art bible: silhouette, palette, water, materials, lighting, outline, damage/reward language and accessibility.
- One base ship rig plus starter appearance and modular hull/sail/flag/emblem/wake/ornament.
- Twelve section templates; Saltwind, Gravewater and Devil's Maw dressing kits; ocean/Dread/portal/Beacon/chest.
- Eight POIs, four temporary powerups, XP/Gold/Item/heal pickups.
- Seven base enemy families, Siren, Fire Eel, two Drowned Pursuit families, three bosses and biome variants.
- Six launch Weapon kits and shared Item/status/proc effects.
- 48 content icons, six Captain portraits, currencies/status/navigation/defense/mastery/event symbols.
- All UI screens/states in UI spec, partner pack, Chronicle/store art, story panels, logo, app icon, key art, capsules, screenshots and trailers.
- Audio: Weapon/enemy/boss/player/pickup/POI/UI families; sea/biome ambience; home/combat/boss/overtime/results music states; mixer limits and low-memory imports.

## Quality rules

Polish comes from anticipation, recoil, hit-stop presentation without simulation delay, impact layers, water response, camera impulse, animation recovery, audio priority, reward choreography and transitions. Provide low/high and reduced-effects variants. Simulation never depends on animation/root motion. Pool reset must clear trails, particles, sounds, materials and state. Do not create a unique 3D model for every Item; icons and reusable effects carry most Items.

## Production order

Representative ship/Shark/Cannon/water/HUD → complete Saltwind slice → shared enemy/attack language → remaining biomes/bosses/Weapons → cosmetics/partner set → narrative/marketing → localization and final accessibility. No bulk asset purchase before representative mobile-web profiling.
