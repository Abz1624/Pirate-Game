# Performance reviews, 2 Oct 2026 (after M7c/M7d)

Asked by the owner: "check how Megabonk is handling enemies and drawing their minimap"; "did you try pooling, garbage
collection, batching and occlusion culling? check if it's working properly or if we're missing anything". Three read-only
reviews (a web research agent and two v5-reviewer passes), checked against the code.

## Megabonk (sources in the research notes below)

Little is public. From mods and decompilation: enemies are ordinary GameObjects with renderers (managed by an
`EnemyManager`, probably pooled), not ECS; the developer called enemies stacking on each other a "happy accident" (cheap,
loose separation); the minimap is a second orthographic camera rendering into a texture, with world-space icon quads. Its
speed seems to come from cheap per-enemy work and tolerating overlap, not special technology. Our minimap (UI markers,
0.14 ms) is cheaper than a second camera pass on a CPU-bound phone: keep it.
Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megabonk; github.com/WafuRuns/MegabonkBetterMinimapMelonLoader; github.com/svind1er/MegabonkMinimap;
github.com/Dsiman/BeeBlocker; eneba.com/hub/news/megabonk-might-destroy-your-marriage.

## Batching and culling: set up correctly

SRP Batcher on (both pipeline assets); dynamic batching off (M3e measured no gain); static batching irrelevant (islands are
spawned at runtime; M3e tried combining: no gain late, +25 MB); GPU Resident Drawer off (needs compute: not on WebGL);
occlusion culling has nothing to bake (runtime stage, top-down sea) and costs nothing; far clip 250 m; LODs on heavy
models; creature animators cull off screen; the shaders that matter are SRP-Batcher compatible. What is left is the number
of renderers and lights (each draw is a JavaScript call on WebGL; ~800 draws at 9:00 in M3e).

## Pooling and GC: mostly pooled; a few real leaks of work

Pooled and fine: pickups, enemy shots, weapon effects, minimap markers, hazard rings, sound (one AudioSource), the
simulation (arrays sized to the cap, no LINQ or allocating physics per frame). The 29 KB/frame in the Editor profile is
mostly the Editor and dev tools (it barely changes from 0 to 80 enemies). On WebGL the GC cannot run incrementally: one
collection was 13.6 ms (Editor). Found:

| # | Where | Problem | Impact (WebGL phone) |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | `Data/Pools/SaltwindPools.asset`, `EnemyViewPresenter.cs` | Enemy view pools prewarm 28 (60 small boats); crowds reach 80-260, so copies are created mid-fight (`Instantiate` 0.24 ms avg, 0.62 ms in slow frames) | High: hitches when waves grow |
| G2 | `VfxParticleUtil.cs`, `ProjectileViewPresenter.cs`, `HostileShotPresenter.cs` | Every cannonball allocates ~10-13 component arrays and name strings, redoes trail setup | High-medium |
| G3 | `PooledEffects.cs`, `CombatFeedbackPresenter.cs`, `VfxProfilePlayer.cs` | Hit effects allocate arrays per play, no per-frame cap; `BroadcastMessage` per play | Medium |
| G4 | `DamageNumberPresenter.cs` | A new string per number; pool can grow to ~800 objects (no cap) | Medium |
| G5 | `AuthoredHudPresenter.cs` | Timer and quest strings rebuilt every frame | Low-medium |
| G6 | `EnemyViewPresenter.cs` (wake cache), `EnemyBurnFlash.cs` (material per burn) | Allocations per spawn and per burn | Low-medium |
| G7 | game code | No `GC.Collect` at natural pauses (level-up cards, results) to move collections out of combat | Medium (hitch placement) |
| G8 | bench builds | The Tuning Console runs every frame in bench builds: phone numbers include a cost release builds lack | Measurement |

## CPU: the enemy simulation (found by the Megabonk comparison, confirmed in code)

| # | Where | Problem | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | `Runtime/Enemies/EnemySimulation.Tick.cs` `ResolveAgentOverlaps` | Compares every pair of enemies, twice, although a spatial hash is built that frame: ~6,300 checks at 80, ~25,000 at 160 | High, grows with the square of the crowd |
| S2 | `Foundation/World/IslandObstacles.cs` (`Depenetrate`), route probes | Physics overlap queries for every enemy every frame, near islands or not | Medium-high (largest part of V4.Sim.Move) |
| S3 | enemy behaviour and view work | Off-screen and far enemies updated every frame | Medium |
