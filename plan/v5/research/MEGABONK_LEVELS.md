# Megabonk weapon levels and power spikes — research, 30 Sep 2026

Collected by a research agent. The official wiki refused the connection, so sources are megabonk.org, megabonkinfo.org,
guides and search snippets; `(unverified)` marks facts seen only in snippets.

- **Slots**: start with 2 weapons and 2 tomes (passives); up to 4 of each, unlocked through quests and the meta shop.
  (megabonk.org/guides/unlocks/weapon-slots)
- **Weapon levels**: each level-up pick is +1 weapon level with **random stat rolls** from that weapon's stat pool, not a
  fixed track. Common rolls 1–2 stats, higher rarities 2. Rarity multiplies the roll: Common ×1.0, Uncommon ×1.2, Rare
  ×1.4, Epic ×1.6, Legendary ×2.0. (gurugamer summary of the Steam upgrades guide; megabonk.org XP Tome)
- **Max levels**: weapons 40, tomes 99; the Legendary item Pot raises the weapon cap, Anvil adds a stat line to every
  later upgrade. Nobody reaches 40 in one stage (roughly 25–40 player levels by 10:00, split over up to 8 slots;
  inference, unverified).
- **No weapon evolutions.** Late power comes from Legendary rarity rolls, Charge Shrines (pick 1 of 3 stat boosts;
  golden ones are Legendary), Moai shrines, chest items, Microwaves (duplicate an item), Boss Curse (extra boss and chest),
  Challenge Shrines (free chest) and tomes with no practical cap. (megabonk.org/guides/mechanics/shrines)
- **Chests** cost gold that rises with each chest; keys give free-chest chances. Items never level; most stack.
- **Stage timeline**: miniboss 3:00, swarm 4:00, swarm 7:00, miniboss 8:00, Final Swarm at 10:00 designed to kill you.
- **Comparison**: Vampire Survivors caps weapons at 8 and evolves at max + passive + a chest after 10:00, and its chests
  also grant 1, 3 or 5 level-ups; Survivor.io has 5 levels and evolves at 5 + passive through a boss or elite chest.

## Options for keeping 12 weapon levels with evolutions reachable by ~minute 7–9

1. Evolution threshold below max (for example level 8 of 12 with the paired item); levels 9–12 continue on the evolved
   weapon. Predictable; the card text must explain it.
2. Rarity cards that grant several levels (Common +1, Rare +2, Legendary +3), Luck pushing rarity up; a pity rule keeps
   variance in check. Megabonk's rarity idea turned into level count.
3. Elite and boss chests that level weapons (1/3/5 levels, VS-style) or deliver the evolution (Survivor.io-style).
4. A gold-bought "shipwright" landmark that adds weapon levels at rising prices.
5. Focused offers (fewer early slots, banish/skip) so cards level owned weapons more often.
6. A steeper XP curve plus a time gate on evolutions.

The researcher's suggested fit: options 2 and 3 together.
