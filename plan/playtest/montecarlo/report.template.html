Saltwind Balance Simulator

The one balance simulator: a numbers-only Monte Carlo of a 15-minute Saltwind voyage. It reads the game's real configs (exported by the Unity Editor from the config set the game runs on) and is calibrated on your five recorded voyages, each replayed on the config it was played on. It measures damage, kills, XP, levels and hull minute by minute; the live simulator below lets you try other settings.

Verdict

What the simulation found

  • Refit on your five voyages, each on its own config (8 Oct, C9a.1). The voyages span four builds: island hits of 10 then 5 hull, XP at 0.5 (old per-piece payout), 0.8 and 0.4, weapon damage before and after D20. Each voyage is now replayed on a snapshot of the config it was played on (two are exact Unity exports with the voyage's config hash), so the fitted constants describe your play, not the config changes. Your last voyage (16:38, today's balance) weighs most.
  • On today's balance the model now contains your voyage: level, kills and survival sit inside the model's 10th-90th percentile band at every minute (level 100%, kills 100%, damage taken 88%, hull 88% of minutes), and your sinking at 16:38 sits at the 39th percentile of the model's sinking times (before: the model never sank, levelled 7 levels faster and took half the damage).
  • Where it still misses: on the three oldest builds (before D20) you killed 20-35% more in the first minutes than the model (and through S2), and on S2-S4 the model's median voyage sinks 3-5 minutes before you did (inside its band, which is wide because of that). Hull right before a sinking runs below the band (the model's per-minute band is for the voyages still afloat).
  • New in the model: enemies that miss the ship and wander off (they hold a place in the crowd, then leave unkilled), damage as whole hits, the storm edge and enemy hazards (measured: about one hit every two minutes), the Gilded Wreck repair, health barrels you miss, the Drowned Pursuit engaging fully, kill-band elites piling up (they get 3% of your damage each), and voyage-to-voyage variation in aim, pickup and exposure.
  • Shooters do half the enemy damage. Your voyages took about half their enemy damage from cannonboats, gunner cogs and needle sloops; the model now lands 16% of shots (was 2%) and half as many rams. Islands stay the largest single source (about a third of the hull lost).
  • Builds decide survival. With your picks on today's balance (Logbook-heavy: Ironclad, Carpenter's Notes), the model's Rook sinks at a median 18:31. With the model's generic picker (a new weapon whenever offered, random Logbooks), all six Captains sink at a median of about 10:50, 43% before 10:00, and 28% beat the boss: the Logbooks you chose are worth about eight minutes.

How the model works

  • One step per second. Enemies spawn from the real timeline: bands and pools, the minute waves' floors (topped up one per spawn gap, up to each wave's limit), release windows, hordes, swarm pulses, the kill band and its elites, minibosses and scheduled elites (arena fights pause the waves until they end), the boss at the Beacon, and the Drowned Pursuit after 15:00. HP, damage and XP are the game's own.
  • Minibosses, elites and the boss get the game's fight-length floor: a number of seconds of the build's theoretical damage per second, computed the way the game computes it.
  • The player's weapons, levels, upgrade cards (offer weights by weapons carried, lines per rarity, axis caps), Logbooks, chest items and Empowered evolutions follow the game's data. XP comes from barrels that burst into pieces, as in the game; health drops come at a kill after a random wait.
  • Fitted to your five voyages (fit_sessions.mjs, each on its own config from session_data.mjs, a proper score on level, kills, damage, hull, elites and enemies alive each minute plus the survival time): 35% of the build's realistic damage lands, 90% of the wave enemies come to fight (the rest wander off for 20 s), a new enemy needs 0.5 s to come into range, you sail over 70% of the XP barrels (at most 100 a minute) and 80% of the health barrels, 45% of your damage goes to an arena fight, 5% to a scheduled elite and 3% to each kill-band elite. Measured from the voyages: island hits 1.4 a minute, storm-edge and hazard hits 0.49 a minute (2.6 hull each), one wreck repair at 5:00. Each voyage draws its own aim and pickup (spread 0.15) and exposure and heal luck (spread 0.8).
  • Incoming damage, in whole hits: melee contacts grow with the crowd, shooters fire every 2.5 s with a 16% hit chance, focus enemies hit at a fixed rate, island hits cost the game's island damage, and the storm edge and hazards hit at the measured rate.
  • Limits: it is a guide, not the game. Points of interest other than chests and the wreck repair, the boss director's attacks and movement are not modelled; the survival band is wide because on the older builds the model sinks earlier than you did. Play is the final check.
