Saltwind Balance Simulator

The one balance simulator: a numbers-only Monte Carlo of a 15-minute Saltwind voyage. It reads the game's real configs (exported by the Unity Editor from the config set the game runs on) and is calibrated on your three recorded voyages. It measures damage, kills, XP, levels and hull minute by minute; the live simulator below lets you try other settings.

Verdict

What the simulation found

  • One simulator, on the game's real configs (7 Oct). The in-engine simulator is retired. This model's data now comes from the Unity Editor (the config set the game runs on, with variants, elite spawners and the XP table resolved the way the game resolves them). The old YAML reader is kept as a fallback, and a check lists any field where the two disagree. That check found three drifts in the old reader, all now fixed: the registry elites (Hammerhead, Kraken Spawn) had their variant's HP and damage instead of their base's, variants had their base's attack and threat, and elite spawners were missing the Rich affix's ×1.25 hull.
  • New rules, as the game plays now: island hits (5 hull, at most one every 2 s, at the rate measured in your voyages); fight-length floors on the game's own theoretical damage per second; arena fights pause the waves until they end; the wave floor tops up at the game's pace; level-up cards from the offer weights by weapons carried (new Logbooks also with six weapons); the 15-minute timeline; timed health drops at kills; the Beacon boss at 14:00 unless a voyage says otherwise.
  • Calibrated on your three voyages. The old model levelled about half as fast, killed half as many enemies, never sank and ended fights in 5 s. Now level, kills, damage taken and hull follow your voyages minute by minute, and the second voyage's sinking at 14:00 is reproduced (62% of the model's runs sink by then). See the calibration table below.
  • Found in the game: XP from a kill depends on the effects quality. An enemy's XP barrel bursts into 3 or more pieces, each worth at least 1 XP, so a 2-XP enemy pays about 4 to 6 XP before the half-XP pace. The number of pieces follows the quality tier's effects density, so the same voyage levels faster on High than on Low. The model fits a density of 0.6.
  • Islands are the biggest source of damage: about 45% of the hull you lost in your voyages, and 43% in the model. Then come Drowned Pursuers (overtime), Cutthroat Skiffs and Drowned Harriers.
  • With your play profile on all six Captains, the median voyage lasts about 18 minutes and 69% beat the boss (summoned at 14:00). 3% sink before 10:00 and 21% before 14:00. The Captains land within about ±10% of each other (16.0 to 18.3 minutes).

Calibration on your recorded voyages

Each voyage beside the model before this change (the old rules and data) and now (the game's configs, the new rules, the fitted constants). The model replays each voyage's level-up picks and is run 200 times; its numbers are the median of the runs still afloat. The three voyages were played on slightly different configs: the first had island hits of 10 hull, so it sank sooner than the model can.

How the model works

  • One step per second. Enemies spawn from the real timeline: bands and pools, the minute waves' floors (topped up one per spawn gap, up to each wave's limit), release windows, hordes, swarm pulses, the kill band and its elites, minibosses and scheduled elites (arena fights pause the waves until they end), the boss at the Beacon, and the Drowned Pursuit after 15:00. HP, damage and XP are the game's own.
  • Minibosses, elites and the boss get the game's fight-length floor: a number of seconds of the build's theoretical damage per second, computed the way the game computes it.
  • The player's weapons, levels, upgrade cards (offer weights by weapons carried, lines per rarity, axis caps), Logbooks, chest items and Empowered evolutions follow the game's data. XP comes from barrels that burst into pieces, as in the game; health drops come at a kill after a random wait.
  • Fitted to your three voyages (fit_sessions.mjs): 40% of the build's realistic damage lands, a new enemy needs 1.5 s to come into range, you sail over 95% of the barrels (at most 140 a minute), 30% of your damage goes to an arena fight, 10% to a scheduled elite and 6% to each kill-band elite. Island hits come 1.45 times a minute, as measured in your voyages.
  • Incoming damage: melee contacts grow with the crowd, shooters fire every 2.5 s with a hit chance, focus enemies hit at a fixed rate, and island hits cost the game's 5 hull.
  • Limits: it is a guide, not the game. Storm, hazards and barrels are not modelled, and your third voyage took more damage than the model gives it. Play is the final check.
